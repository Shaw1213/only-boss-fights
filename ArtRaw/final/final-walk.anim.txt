A slow, heavy, menacing walk toward the right, walking on the spot: he stays in the same place in the frame and does not travel. One armored foot steps forward, then the other; knees bend a little, at least one foot always touches the ground, no running, no jumping. He has exactly one black sword, held low in his near hand, pointing forward and down; the sword arm does not swing. Exactly one huge clawed tentacle arm hangs forward from his far shoulder and sways slowly with the steps, claws open. The coiled tentacles around his body drift slightly. Helm and crown stay level; the glowing eye on his chest stays the same.
