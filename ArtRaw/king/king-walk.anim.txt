A slow, heavy, regal walk toward the right, walking on the spot: the king stays in the same place in the frame and does not travel. One armored foot steps forward, then the other; knees bend a little, at least one foot always touches the ground, no running, no jumping. He has exactly one sword: the pale ice-blue longsword stays still, held low in his near hand, pointing forward and down; the sword arm does not swing. Helm, crown and torso stay level; the red plume sways slightly.
