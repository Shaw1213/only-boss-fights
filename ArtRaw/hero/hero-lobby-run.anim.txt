A light, relaxed jog toward the right, running on the spot: he stays in the same place in the frame and does not travel. Exactly two running steps in the loop, evenly timed, four frames each: in the first step his left foot reaches forward, lands and pushes off; in the second step his right foot. Knees lift moderately, a slight forward lean, head steady. The arms swing clearly and loosely from the shoulders, opposite to the legs, with relaxed open hands, not fists: when his left foot reaches forward, his right hand swings forward to chest height and his left hand swings back behind his hip; when his right foot reaches forward, his left hand comes forward to chest height and his right hand swings back behind his hip. Between the steps both hands pass down beside his hips. No hand is ever raised above his chest. The sheathed sword stays strapped on his back the whole time and does not move; the coat flaps slightly behind him.
