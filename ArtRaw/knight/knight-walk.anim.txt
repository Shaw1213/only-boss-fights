A slow, heavy, menacing walk toward the right, walking on the spot: the knight stays in the same place in the frame and does not travel. One armored foot steps forward, then the other; knees bend a little, at least one foot always touches the ground, no running, no jumping. The longsword stays still, held low in his near hand, pointing forward and down; the sword arm does not swing. Helm and torso stay level; the red plume sways slightly.
